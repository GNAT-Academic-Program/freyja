# PCB stackup, routing rules and impedance

`python3 tools/gen_pcb.py --rules-only` regenerates the layer list, the
stackup, the project net classes and `kicad/odin.kicad_dru` while preserving
placement and routing. Without that flag the generator creates an empty
board; never run it that way on the live board.

## Selected fabrication stackup

**JLC08161H-1080A**, 8 layers, nominal 1.6 mm (1.624 mm template total),
1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner, ENIG: **S / GND / S / PWR / GND / S / GND / S**.
Source: JLCPCB's impedance-template API as published in
[gsuberland/jlcpcb_autogenerated_stackups](https://github.com/gsuberland/jlcpcb_autogenerated_stackups)
(normalised JSON, retrieved 2026-10-23, saved as
[jlc08161h-1080a-stackup.json](jlc08161h-1080a-stackup.json)). Order this
template by name and ask JLC to confirm the production geometry.

| Layer | Role | Dielectric below | Thickness, mm | Material | Dk |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| F.Cu (L1) | signal, components, 100 Ω pairs, RGMII | prepreg | 0.0764 | NP-155F 1080 RC67% | 3.91 |
| In1.Cu (L2) | **GND** | core | 0.1000 | NP-155F core | 4.36 |
| In2.Cu (L3) | signal (BGA escape) | prepreg stack | 0.2104 + 0.084 + 0.2104 | 7628 RC49% / 1080 RC67% / 7628 RC49% | 4.40 / 3.91 / 4.40 |
| In3.Cu (L4) | **PWR** planes | core | 0.1000 | NP-155F core | 4.36 |
| In4.Cu (L5) | **GND** | prepreg stack | 0.2104 + 0.084 + 0.2104 | 7628 / 1080 / 7628 | 4.40 / 3.91 / 4.40 |
| In5.Cu (L6) | signal (BGA escape) | core | 0.1000 | NP-155F core | 4.36 |
| In6.Cu (L7) | **GND** | prepreg | 0.0764 | NP-155F 1080 RC67% | 3.91 |
| B.Cu (L8) | signal, no components | — | | | |

Why this template: the outer 1080 prepreg is the same 0.0764 mm / Dk 3.91 as
the JLC06161H-1080B the board was first calculated on, so the saved 100 Ω
solver result still applies; and each 0.1 mm core puts a routing layer (L3,
L6) tight against a ground plane and the power plane (L4) tight against a
ground plane (L5). Copper: 0.035 mm outer, 0.0152 mm inner, as JLC's tables
give them. The board is assembled on one side; B.Cu is a routing layer.

Copper plus dielectric is 1.6236 mm; solder mask adds 0.0254 mm a face and is not
in the template. The PCB holds the two thick prepreg stacks as KiCad sublayers, so F.Cu
to B.Cu is 1.5886 mm mid-plane to mid-plane, which is the length DRC counts for a through
via. The general thickness field stays the nominal 1.6 mm and does not set that length.
`tools/check_stackup.py` checks what pcbnew loads and saves against the template; the
gate runs it.

## 100 Ω differential: LVDS link pairs and PHY MDI pairs

Coated edge-coupled microstrip on **F.Cu referenced to In1.Cu** or **B.Cu
referenced to In6.Cu**, both planes continuous under the pairs. Solver:
JLC's `DiffEdgeCoupledCoatedMicrostrip1B` on the 0.0764 mm 1080 prepreg,
inputs and responses saved in
[jlc06161h-1080b-impedance.json](jlc06161h-1080b-impedance.json)
(same dielectric, retrieved 2026-09-15):

| Solver input | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| H1 | 0.0764 mm |
| Er1 | 3.91 |
| Finished trace thickness T1 | 1.6 mil (0.04064 mm) |
| Etch taper W1 − W2 | 0.5 mil (0.0127 mm) |
| Mask C1 / C2 / C3 | 1.0 / 0.6 / 1.0 mil |
| Mask CEr | 3.8 |
| Pair gap S1 | 0.200 mm |
| Target | 100 Ω |

Inverse calculation: W1 = 3.8828125 mil = 0.0986 mm, 100.0068 Ω. KiCad:
width **0.099 mm**, gap **0.200 mm**; forward check at the rounded width
99.85 Ω. Above JLC's 3.5 mil (0.0889 mm) minimum.

## 50 Ω single-ended: RGMII

No JLC solver run exists for this line yet. Hammerstad-Jensen with Wheeler's
thickness correction on the same prepreg (H 0.0764 mm, Er 3.91, T 0.04064 mm,
no mask) gives 52.6 Ω at 0.12 mm, 50.6 Ω at 0.13 mm, 48.7 Ω at 0.14 mm;
solder mask lowers a microstrip a few ohm, so the class is set to
**0.125 mm**. This is an estimate: run JLC's single-ended coated-microstrip
calculator with the inputs above before ordering and correct `RGMII_WIDTH`
in `tools/gen_pcb.py` if it disagrees. The field solve below gives 48.4 Ω outer and 49.1 Ω
inner at 0.125 mm. RGMII timing is set by the derived delay limits, not by
millimetres: each group is checked against its own clock in
[`gateware/rgmii1000/`](../gateware/rgmii1000/README.md), where the routed
data-to-clock delays are shown with the FPGA package included. The PHY's
strapped 2 ns delays supply the clock skew; no serpentine on the clock.

## 50 Ω single-ended: SDRAM, target 100 MHz CL3

A 2D field solve of the stackup above gives, at the 0.125 mm class width: F.Cu and B.Cu 48.4 Ω,
5.88 ps/mm; In2 and In5 49.1 Ω, 6.97 ps/mm. The same solver gives 100.3 Ω for the saved JLC pair.
Width ±0.0125 mm moves the inner figure to 46.6–51.9 Ω. This is a calculation, not a fabricator
result.

The limits below are not length-matching rules. They hold only with this scheme: IOB registers on
one 100 MHz clock, `SDRAM_CLK` from an ODDR at +270°, read capture in the IOB on the same clock at
edge CL + 1, bus at 8 mA slow, clock at 12 mA fast through R151. They come from Vivado STA on the
exact part and an AMD IBIS flight simulation, with assumptions listed in [findings](../findings.md):
X4 jitter, U32 output behaviour and U32 input clamps are not verified.

| Timing group | Allowed board delay, ns | On the board, ns |
|---|---|---|
| A, BA, command, CKE, DQM | settle − clock ≤ +4.22; leave − clock ≥ −1.77 | +1.58; −0.36 |
| Write DQ | settle − clock ≤ +4.26; leave − clock ≥ −1.77 | +1.20; −0.39 |
| Read DQ | clock + DQ ≤ 2.17 | 0.60–0.89 |

"Settle" and "leave" are simulated flight times to the last and first valid-level crossings at
U32, including driver and package; a new route must be simulated, not converted from length. The
limits are for 100 MHz only. Do not tune lengths to meet a group's longest net.
The contract and how to rerun it are in [`gateware/sdram100/`](../gateware/sdram100/README.md).

## Generated classes and DRC constraints

| Class | Nets | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default | other nets | 0.15 mm clearance, 0.2 mm track, 0.45/0.20 mm via |
| LVDS_100R | `LINK_*`, `HDMI_CLK_*`, `HDMI_D0`–`D2_*` | outer layers only; 0.099 mm width; 0.200 mm coupled gap; ≤ 0.1 mm intra-pair skew; pair tracks ≥ 0.31 mm from other copper, with the one J73 exception below |
| MDI_100R | `ETH_MDI*` | same geometry and rules as LVDS_100R |
| RGMII | `PHY_TXC`, `PHY_TXD?`, `PHY_TX_CTL`, `PHY_TXC_U30`, `PHY_TXD?_U30`, `PHY_TX_CTL_U30`, `PHY_RXC`, `PHY_RXD?`, `PHY_RX_CTL` | 0.125 mm width |
| SDRAM | `SDRAM_*` | 0.125 mm width; timing limits above |
| PSRAM | `PSRAM*`, including chip-side `_MEM` nets | 0.1 mm minimum and preferred width |
| AUX_5A | `AUX_RAIL` | 2.0 mm width, 1.0/0.5 mm vias |
| PWR_3A | `VBUS`, `VBUS_SS`, `VSYS`, `VIN_EXT`, `+5V_BUCK` | 1.5 mm width, 0.8/0.4 mm vias; the U11 sense, U34, J1, U18, U26 and monitor-tap exceptions below |
| EXT_2A | `+5V_EXT`, `+3V3_EXT`, `VCCIO_1_EXT`, `VCCIO_2_EXT` | 1.0 mm width, 0.8/0.4 mm vias |

Skew is checked within each P/N pair. It sums track length and via barrels between
the connected layers, as lengths: it knows no layer delay and cannot see a through-hole
contact's barrel, so it is not a delay check. The 0.31 mm clearance (about four
dielectric heights) keeps other copper from loading the non-coplanar model. It
holds a pair track against any foreign copper, pads included, so a track
entering fine-pitch pads can fall under it.

**J73 exceptions.** At 0.5 mm pitch a 0.099 mm track on a J73 pad centre is
0.3005 mm from the next pad, so 0.31 mm cannot be met, and P and N are 1.0 mm
apart with a GND pad between, so the gap there is 1.0 − 0.099 = 0.901 mm. Two
rules cover this, on F.Cu only, for a track on an `HDMI_CLK_*` or `HDMI_D0`–`D2_*`
net wholly inside the board's `J73_TMDS_ENTRY` rule area (x 234.95–240.85, y
214.19–215.40): `HDMI TMDS entry to J73 pads` allows 0.300 mm against a J73 pad,
not between coupled partners; `HDMI TMDS entry pair gap at J73` allows a pair
gap of 0.200–0.901 mm. Width, coupled gap, skew and 0.31 mm everywhere else are
unchanged. Without the area the rules never apply and the entries fail. They say
nothing about HDMI electrical compliance, which remains unverified.

**J71/J72 exception.** A 0.099 mm track on a south-row contact's axis passes two
north-row contacts with 0.2085 mm a side. `LINK entry to J71 J72 pads` allows 0.200 mm
for a `LINK_*` track wholly inside the board's `J71_LINK_ENTRY` or `J72_LINK_ENTRY`
rule area, against those jacks' pads only. The pair-gap rule is unchanged. Contacts
3-6 straddle 4-5, so each jack has F entries (TXC, RXC) and B entries (TXD, RXD);
both members of a pair enter on one layer. See [findings](../findings.md).

**U30 exceptions.** U30's 0.2 mm pads on 0.4 mm pitch leave 0.2505 mm from a track on
a pad centre to the next pad. Three rules cover an `ETH_MDI*` track wholly inside the
board's F.Cu `U30_MDI_LAUNCH` rule area: `MDI launch to U30 pads` allows 0.17 mm against
U30's pads only; `MDI launch to other MDI pairs` allows 0.16 mm against a track or via
of another MDI pair, not coupled partners; `MDI launch to PHY_1V0 exits` allows 0.27 mm
against a `PHY_1V0` track also wholly inside the area, for supply pins 3 and 8. A
`PHY_1V0` via, every other net, and the same tracks outside the area keep 0.31 mm. See
[findings](../findings.md).

**U11 sense exception.** `+5V_BUCK` is in PWR_3A, and 1.5 mm holds for its load path:
L2, the output capacitors and the way on to U34. U11's feedback sense branch is on the
same net and carries the divider's 40 uA. `U11 output sense branch` allows 0.2 mm for a
`+5V_BUCK` track on F.Cu wholly inside the board's `U11_SENSE` rule area, a strip 0.5 mm
wide from C189.1 under U11 to R55.1 (x 226.025–233.0, y 116.325–124.4). Nothing 1.5 mm
wide fits in the strip. Clearances are unchanged; every other PWR_3A net, `+5V_BUCK` on
any other layer and the same track outside the area keep 1.5 mm. Without the area the
rule never applies and the branch fails. The rule follows `input path 3A` in the file
because KiCad lets the later rule win. See [findings](../findings.md).

**U34 exceptions (2026-11-09).** U34's IN1 (`VBUS`) and IN2 (`+5V_BUCK`) are 0.40 x 1.05 mm
pins with a pin of another net 0.30 mm away on both sides, so no 1.5 mm copper can touch
them. `U34 input pin necks` allows 0.5 mm for a `VBUS` or `+5V_BUCK` track on F.Cu wholly
inside `U34_NECK_IN1` or `U34_NECK_IN2`, the pin's own neck into the 1.5 mm copper and the
0.8 mm via beside it. `U34 VBUS divider taps` allows 0.25 mm for `VBUS` wholly inside
`U34_VBUS_TAPS` (F.Cu stubs into R162.1 and R164.1) or `U34_VBUS_TAPS_IN2` (the In2 hop from
the IN1 via): the two 100k dividers draw 50 uA each. `VIN_EXT tap to Q7's divider` allows
0.2 mm for `VIN_EXT` wholly inside `VIN_EXT_TAP`, the 0.5 mm strip on F.Cu and In5 from D8.1
to R201.1 (0.2 mA at 21 V). Every other track on those nets, the same tracks outside the
areas and every other PWR_3A net keep 1.5 mm; clearances are unchanged. Tests: evidence
`~/freyja-review/u34-2026-11-09/reports/ruletests.txt`.

**Input-tree exceptions (2026-11-09).** Pin and divider geometry again, each a rule
area the board owns and a rule after the U34 ones. `J1 VBUS pin necks`: `VBUS` on F.Cu
wholly inside `J1_VBUS_NECK_W` or `J1_VBUS_NECK_E` may be 0.6 mm; J1's 0.60 x 1.45 mm
`VBUS` pads have GND 0.20 mm and a CC pad 0.25 mm beside them, and the areas allow at
most 0.875 mm of neck beyond the pad (0.27 and 0.18 mm built). `U18 VIN pin neck`:
0.28 mm inside `U18_VIN_NECK` for the HUSB238's 0.28 x 0.67 mm pin on 0.5 mm pitch, at
most 0.98 mm. `U26 IN pin necks`: 0.28 mm inside `U26_IN_NECKS` for the TPS25942A's IN
pins 9 and 10, at most 0.77 mm; `U26 OUT pin necks`: `VBUS_SS` 0.28 mm inside
`U26_OUT_NECKS` for OUT pins 7 and 8, one track along their tips into the 1.5 mm bar.
`U26 VBUS divider tap`: 0.2 mm inside `U26_VBUS_TAP`, R50's 33 uA. `VSYS monitor tap`:
`VSYS` 0.2 mm inside `VSYS_MON_TAP` on F.Cu and In2, R81's 80 uA. The U34 neck areas
`U34_NECK_IN1` and `U34_NECK_IN2` are shrunk to the tracks: at most 0.73 and 0.82 mm of
neck beyond the pad (0.37 and 0.58 mm built). Every other track on those nets, the same
tracks outside the areas and every other PWR_3A net keep 1.5 mm; clearances are
unchanged. Tests: evidence `~/freyja-review/input-2026-11-09/reports/ruletests.txt`.

Inner-layer tracks are prohibited for the 100 Ω classes because the geometry
is calculated only for the outer layers. Plane transitions still need ground
stitching vias beside the signal vias. Board minimum track width is
0.0889 mm; default vias are 0.45 mm pad / 0.20 mm drill (JLC multilayer
minimum 0.30 / 0.15). The `MGT_100R` class is gone with the transceivers.
