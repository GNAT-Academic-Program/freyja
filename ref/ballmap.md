## The constraint that shapes everything

This package has **three HR I/O banks** — 14, 15 and 34 — 150 user I/O total.
`VCCO` is per bank, so **three is the hard ceiling on independent I/O voltage
domains**, and one of them is spoken for.

| Bank | HR I/O | Diff pairs | MRCC | SRCC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14 | 50 | 24 | 4 | 4 |
| 15 | 50 | 24 | 4 | 4 |
| 34 | 50 | 24 | 4 | 4 |

**Bank 14 is not free.** It carries the master-SPI config flash pins and the
`PUDC_B` strap, so its `VCCO` is pinned at 3.3V by the flash. That makes it
the home for everything else fixed at 3.3V — PSRAM, the supervisor sideband,
and the level-shifted slot L — and leaves **banks 15 and 34 as the only two
settable domains**, four slots each.

## Allocation

| Bank | VCCO | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| 14 | **fixed 3.3V** | config flash, PSRAM x4, supervisor sideband, slot L + its 2 direction pins |
| 15 | **settable** 3.3 / 2.5 / 1.8 | slots A B C D |
| 34 | **settable** 3.3 / 2.5 / 1.8 | slots E F G H |

## Extension slots

Ten I/O per slot as **five differential pairs**, one per connector column,
so `P+`/`P-` are vertically adjacent on the header. Single-ended users just
call them `IO0..IO9`.

| Slot | Bank | P0+ | P0- | P1+ | P1- | P2+ | P2- | P3+ | P3- | P4+ | P4- |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L** | 14 | U14 | V14 | V12 | V13 | T12 | U12 | U11 | V11 | U9 | V9 |
| **A** | 15 | D8 | C8 | D9 | C9 | B9 | A9 | C11 | B11 | B10 | A10 |
| **B** | 15 | D11 | C12 | B12 | A12 | A13 | A14 | C14 | B15 | B14 | A15 |
| **C** | 15 | D13 | C13 | E13 | D14 | E15 | D15 | E16 | D16 | B16 | A17 |
| **D** | 15 | C16 | B17 | E17 | D18 | C17 | C18 | G17 | F18 | H16 | G16 |
| **E** | 34 | K6 | K5 | J5 | J4 | K2 | K1 | K3 | L2 | L4 | L3 |
| **F** | 34 | L5 | M5 | M2 | M1 | M6 | N6 | N1 | P1 | M4 | N4 |
| **G** | 34 | N3 | N2 | P4 | P3 | R2 | R1 | R3 | T2 | U2 | U1 |
| **H** | 34 | V3 | V2 | T4 | T3 | U4 | V4 | P6 | P5 | U6 | U5 |

Slot L is buffered; A–H are direct. Slot L additionally uses L14, U10 for the two translator direction controls, so it costs **12** I/O for 10 connector signals.
