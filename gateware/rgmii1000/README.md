# RGMII profile: 1000 Mb/s, 125 MHz DDR, 2.5 V

Timing contract for U30 (RTL8211FDI-CG) on the routed board, with `TXDLY = 1` and `RXDLY = 1` strapped.
It is worst-case analysis on committed copper: nothing here has been measured on hardware. `ref/freya.xdc`,
`tools/alloc.py` and `gateware/gate/` do not read this directory, and the allocation gate is unchanged. This
is a timing fixture, not a MAC.

**Status: RX calculated positive with named allowances. TX provisional:** Realtek publishes neither the
tolerance of the internal TXC delay nor an input window for `TXDLY = 1`, so the TX limit below is a reading of
Table 60, not a guarantee. Both directions pass in Vivado against those limits.

## What a design must do

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| TX registers | TXD[3:0], TX_CTL and TXC each from an ODDR in the IOB on one 125 MHz clock, with no phase shift. TXC: D1 = 1, D2 = 0. `run.tcl` fails unless 6 ODDR sit in OLOGIC. |
| TX drive | All six LVCMOS25, `DRIVE 8`, `SLEW FAST`. |
| RX capture | RXC on its MRCC ball: IBUF, then BUFIO to the IDDRs and BUFR to the fabric. RXD[3:0] and RX_CTL each through a fixed IDELAYE2, **11 taps**, into an IDDR in the IOB. `run.tcl` fails unless 5 IDDR sit in ILOGIC. |
| IDELAYCTRL | 200 MHz reference from an MMCM output, as in `top.v`. The tap value holds only at 200 MHz. |
| Constraints | `rgmii1000_timing.xdc`, generated by `mkprofile.py`. It refers to `txc_oddr/C`; a design with another cell name changes that one line. |
| After rerouting any of these nets | Rerun `route.py`, then `mkprofile.py` and the fixture. Delay limits do not convert from length here, because the routes mix layers and the package terms differ by up to 97 ps within a group. |

## How each row of Table 60 was used

Rev 1.7, section 10.6.2. The strap is read from `tools/design.py`: RXD1 and RXD0 pull to `+2V5`.

| Direction | Mode | Row used | Meaning here |
|---|---|---|---|
| TX, FPGA to U30 | `TXDLY = 1`: U30 delays TXC inside itself | TskewT ±0.5 ns, "without delay integrated" | The FPGA launches clock and data together. The row is defined at the transmitter; it is held here at U30's pins, so the board has to fit inside it too. |
| RX, U30 to FPGA | `RXDLY = 1`: U30 is the transmitter with the delay integrated | TsetupT and TholdT, 1.2 ns minimum, at U30's pins | Guaranteed data window. Input delay max = T/2 − 1.2 + (data − clock)hi; min = 1.2 + (data − clock)lo. Independent of period and duty, since both figures reference the same edge. |

Not used, deliberately:

- **TsetupR and TholdR, 1.0 ns.** These describe a receiver when the *transmitter* holds the delay. U30 as receiver is in the other mode, and the FPGA's own window comes from Vivado.
- **TskewR, 1.0 to 2.6 ns, and "1.5 to 2.0 ns of trace".** That is the delay-on-the-board mode. No 2 ns serpentine is wanted; adding one with `TXDLY = 1` would double the delay.
- **The nominal 2 ns itself.** It appears nowhere in the constraints. As an inference only: if the receiver's own window is TskewR and the internal delay lies within the 1.5 to 2.0 ns the table asks of a board, the allowed skew at the pins works out as −0.5 to +0.6 ns, which is why ±0.5 ns is a sensible reading. Realtek does not state it.

## Limits and result

`data − clock` is at the receiver's pins, in ns, including the board and the FPGA package. The limit is the slack of the
run with no board term, which is the room the board may use. Setup and hold are the run with the routed terms, worst of the
Slow and Fast corners, both DDR edges (`results/edges.txt`). RX includes a ±0.1 ns allowance, so the RX limits are 0.1 ns
tighter in practice.

| Net | Limit | Routed | Setup | Hold |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| TXD0 | −0.249 to +0.159 | −0.085 to −0.052 | +0.211 | +0.164 |
| TXD1 | −0.252 to +0.156 | −0.044 to −0.013 | +0.169 | +0.208 |
| TXD2 | −0.216 to +0.193 | −0.110 to −0.076 | +0.269 | +0.106 |
| TXD3 | −0.221 to +0.188 | −0.064 to −0.032 | +0.220 | +0.157 |
| TX_CTL | −0.206 to +0.203 | −0.106 to −0.074 | +0.277 | **+0.100** |
| RXD0 | −0.484 to +0.308 | +0.001 to +0.004 | +0.204 | +0.385 |
| RXD1 | −0.389 to +0.397 | −0.078 to −0.075 | +0.372 | +0.211 |
| RXD2 | −0.474 to +0.311 | +0.041 to +0.046 | +0.165 | +0.415 |
| RXD3 | −0.498 to +0.292 | +0.035 to +0.043 | **+0.149** | +0.433 |
| RX_CTL | −0.496 to +0.294 | −0.069 to −0.064 | +0.258 | +0.327 |

TX and RX are matched each to its own clock and not to each other. TX is limited by the Slow corner on both checks, RX
setup by Fast and RX hold by Slow.

## Choices that were tested

**RX capture.** Direct BUFIO capture with no IDELAY fails hold by 1.02 ns (Slow), because BUFIO insertion delays the clock
past the window: setup +2.87, hold −1.02. A fixed IDELAY moves the data instead. At 16 taps setup is −0.146 (Fast) and hold
+0.731 (Slow); 78 ps a tap puts the balance near 11, which this profile uses. No phase search was run: one calibration,
then the check.

**TX drive and slew.** `results/drive.txt`, on the routed TXC. Table 60 limits TXC to 0.75 ns, 20 to 80 %.

| Option | Slow corner edge, ns | Fast corner, V | Verdict |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 4 mA fast | 2.38 | clean | Too slow |
| 8 mA slow on TXC | 1.06 | | Too slow on TXC's falling edge |
| **8 mA fast** | **0.63** | 4.13, −1.48, ringback −0.31 | **Used** |
| 12 mA fast | 0.63 | 4.13, −2.03, ringback −0.92 | Rings harder |
| 12 mA slow | 0.69 | 3.96, −1.77, ringback −0.62 | Rings harder |

Using 8 mA slow on data alone, with 8 mA fast on TXC, fails the TX setup limit by 0.536 ns in the Slow corner, because the
slow buffers' asymmetry reaches the ±0.5 ns window. The SDRAM profile's 8 mA slow bus is therefore not reused here.

## Assumptions and what is not established

- **U30's receiver in `TXDLY = 1`:** no tolerance for the 2 ns and no input window published. This is the missing term that keeps TX provisional.
- **Fast-corner ringing on TX at U30:** up to 4.13 V and −1.48 V with no clamps modelled, against Table 54's −0.2 to 2.8 V rating for the 2.5 V RGMII supply (no separate input rating is listed), and ringback up to 0.31 V inside VIH 1.7 V and VIL 0.7 V. It stays clear of mid-supply, so it does not cross 1.25 V. Upper estimates: U30's input clamps and capacitance are unpublished, 2 to 5 pF is an assumption, and there is no series resistor on the board.
- **U30's output into the real line:** no IBIS model. Covered by the ±0.1 ns allowance, which is a judgement rather than a figure.
- **IDELAY pattern-dependent jitter:** not in Vivado's report and not taken from a data sheet here, since DS181 is not in the repository. It comes out of the RX margin.
- **Jitter:** X4 (C717684) is assumed at 200 ps peak to peak, as in the SDRAM profile; Table 60 gives no RXC jitter.
- **Via delays:** barrels at 6.97 ps/mm, assumed, with no via stub or return-path effect. Layer delays are from the field solve in `../sdram100/results/fieldsolve.txt`.
- **Not assessed:** crosstalk between lanes at 0.28 mm pitch, simultaneous-switching noise, and 10/100 modes, where Table 60's skew limits do not apply.
- **Hardware:** none of this has been measured.

## Reproduce

```sh
source /tools/Xilinx/2026.1/Vivado/settings64.sh
git show HEAD:kicad/odin.kicad_pcb > /tmp/b.kicad_pcb       # the committed board, not a working copy
python3 gateware/rgmii1000/route.py /tmp/b.kicad_pcb        # data/route.json; 4 minutes
python3 gateware/rgmii1000/drive.py /tmp/b.kicad_pcb > gateware/rgmii1000/results/drive.txt
python3 gateware/rgmii1000/mkprofile.py /tmp/lim --no-board # no-board fixture
python3 gateware/rgmii1000/mkprofile.py                     # top.v, *.xdc
vivado -mode batch -nojournal -nolog -source gateware/rgmii1000/run.tcl -tclargs /tmp/lim
vivado -mode batch -nojournal -nolog -source gateware/rgmii1000/run.tcl
python3 gateware/rgmii1000/margins.py /tmp/lim gateware/rgmii1000/build
```

Run Vivado from a scratch directory: it leaves `.Xil/` and `clockInfo.txt` there. Products go to `build/` (ignored).
